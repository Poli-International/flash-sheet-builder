## What is Tattoo Flash Sheet Builder?

Tattoo Flash Sheet Builder is a free web tool that lets tattoo artists and studios create professional flash sheets without design software. You add each flash design with its name, style, size, price, and notes. The tool builds a printable client-ready sheet and lets you export a CSV for your records. All data stays in your browser using local storage, so nothing is sent to any server.

The tool includes artist info fields for studio name, contact details, and sheet date. You can mark designs as sold, delete individual pieces, or clear the entire list. The print preview shows a clean layout with design numbers, names, styles, sizes, prices, and notes. Sold designs display with a SOLD label.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Tattoo artists** who want a quick way to present available flash to walk-in clients without firing up design software.

**Studio managers** who need to track which flash designs are available, sold, and their total value.

**Guest artists** preparing flash for conventions or guest spots who need a clean printable sheet.

**Apprentices** learning how to organize and present flash offerings professionally.

**Shop owners** who want a simple digital record of flash inventory that can be exported to spreadsheets.

## How to Use

### Step 1 - Enter Artist Information

Type your name or studio name in the Artist / Studio Name field. Add your contact or Instagram handle in the Contact / Instagram field. The Sheet Date field automatically fills with today's date, but you can change it.

### Step 2 - Add Flash Designs

Fill in the design form:
- **Design Name** - Enter the name of your flash design, for example "Serpent Rose"
- **Style** - Select from Traditional, Neo-Traditional, Blackwork, Fine Line, Geometric, Watercolour, Realism, Japanese, Tribal, Script / Lettering, Minimalist, or Other
- **Size** - Choose Micro (under 3 cm), Small (3-5 cm), Medium (5-10 cm), Large (10-20 cm), or XL (20 cm+)
- **Price** - Enter the price in pounds, for example 80
- **Notes** - Add optional details like colour, black and grey, placement, or other info

Click the Add Design button. The design appears in the list below.

### Step 3 - Manage Your Design List

The design list shows each piece with its number, name, style, size, price, status, and notes. Use the Sold button to mark a design as sold. Use the × button to delete a design. The summary strip at the bottom shows total count, available count, sold count, and total value of available designs.

### Step 4 - Preview and Print

Click Preview & Print to open the print modal. The preview shows a clean layout with your artist info at the top and each design in a card format. Sold designs show a SOLD label. Click Print to send to your printer, or Close to return to editing.

### Step 5 - Export Data

Click Export CSV to download a CSV file with all your designs. The file includes columns for number, name, style, size, price, status, and notes. Open it in any spreadsheet application for further analysis or record keeping.

## Use Case Examples

### Walk-in Flash Sheet for a Saturday

An artist prepares for a busy Saturday walk-in day. They add five designs:
- Design 1: "Dagger Rose" - Traditional - Medium - £80 - Colour
- Design 2: "Geometric Wolf" - Geometric - Large - £150 - Blackwork
- Design 3: "Fine Line Fern" - Fine Line - Small - £60 - B&G
- Design 4: "Neo-Trad Skull" - Neo-Traditional - Medium - £100 - Colour
- Design 5: "Script Mom" - Script / Lettering - Small - £70 - Any placement

They print the sheet and place it on the counter. When a client picks the Geometric Wolf, they click Sold on that row. The summary updates to show 4 available, 1 sold, total available value £310.

### Convention Flash Collection

A guest artist traveling to a convention prepares 12 flash designs. They enter their Instagram handle as contact info. They use the notes field to indicate which designs work best on arms versus legs. At the convention, they print fresh sheets each morning. When a design sells, they mark it sold. At the end of the convention, they export the CSV to see which styles sold best.

### Studio Flash Inventory Update

A studio manager reviews the current flash inventory. They clear the old list and add 20 new designs from three different artists. Each artist's name goes in the notes field. The manager exports the CSV to share with the shop owner. The summary shows total value of available designs for insurance purposes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is this tool free to use?
Yes, Tattoo Flash Sheet Builder is completely free. No account, sign-up, or payment required.

### 2. Where is my data stored?
All your flash designs and artist info are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server or stored online.

### 3. Will I lose my data if I close the browser?
Your data stays saved in your browser until you clear it. It persists across sessions on the same device and browser.

### 4. How do I clear all designs and start over?
Click the Clear All button. A confirmation prompt appears before deleting everything.

### 5. Can I edit a design after adding it?
The current version does not support inline editing. To change a design, delete it and add a corrected version.

### 6. What formats can I export?
You can export a CSV file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application.

### 7. How do I print the flash sheet?
Click Preview & Print to see the print layout, then click Print to send it to your printer.

### 8. Can I mark a design as sold?
Yes, click the Sold button next to any design. The design shows SOLD in the status column and on the print preview. Click Unsell to revert it.

### 9. What currency does the price field use?
The price field uses pounds (£). The tool displays prices with the £ symbol.

### 10. Does the tool work on mobile devices?
Yes, the tool is responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

### 11. Can I add more than one design at a time?
You add designs one at a time. Each click of Add Design saves and clears the form for the next entry.

### 12. What happens if I don't enter a design name?
The tool shows an alert asking you to enter a design name before saving.
